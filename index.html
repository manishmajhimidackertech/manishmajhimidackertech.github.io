Projects

Tunnel Trouble 3D

Pilot a small ship through an endless, twisting tunnel. Slip through the gaps in the doors, dodge the bars and, in Mine Mode, steer around drifting mines.

  • Classic and Mine modes, best score kept per mode
  • Four tunnel styles: Reactor, Laboratory, Bunker, Catacomb
  • Touch joystick, keyboard or gamepad
  • Synthwave soundtrack generated with WebAudio
  • Three.js
  • Vite
  • PWA
  • WebAudio

Hostile Horizon

A side-scrolling aerial combat game. Shoot down fighters and gunships, bomb tanks and flak guns, take out the boss at the end of each sortie and upgrade your jet in the hangar.

  • Five theatres, from Verdant Valley to Night Siege
  • Bosses: the B-9 Stratofortress and the Leviathan Airship
  • Cannons, homing missiles and gravity bombs
  • Six upgrade tracks, saved progress, offline play
  • Three.js
  • Vite
  • PWA
  • Workbox
▶ Play Source

Both games are installable Progressive Web Apps: open one on your phone and choose “Add to Home Screen” to play it like a native app, even without a connection.
