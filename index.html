Tunnel Arcade

Endless 3D tunnel flyer

Pilot a small ship through an endless, twisting tunnel. Slip through the gaps in the doors, dodge the bars and, in Mine Mode, steer around drifting mines. The tunnel curves, rolls and changes style the deeper you fly, and the pace keeps building. Inspired by the mobile classic Tunnel Trouble 3D.

  • Slow-motion pickups. Grab the glowing hourglass to ease the world down to under half speed for a few seconds. Each one sits on a line that's guaranteed safe to fly through the next obstacle.
  • Tilt steering on phones and tablets, alongside the floating joystick, keyboard and gamepad.
  • Doors with triangle, half, slot, cross, circle and spoke openings, plus striped bars. Spinning versions unlock as you speed up.
  • Four styles modelled on the original: Reactor, Laboratory, Bunker and Catacomb. Best score kept per mode.
  • LED dot-matrix “3-2-1-GO!” countdown and a synthwave soundtrack synthesised with WebAudio.

Hostile Horizon

Side-scrolling aerial combat

Fly a jet through enemy territory. Shoot down fighters and gunships, bomb tanks, flak guns and warships, and take out the boss at the end of each sortie. Spend what you earn on new aircraft and upgrades in the hangar. Inspired by the classic Flash game Hostile Skies.

  • Eight theatres, each with its own terrain and weather: Verdant Valley, Red Canyon, Coastal Assault, Frozen Front, Storm Front, Volcano Ridge, Metropolis and Night Siege.
  • Six aircraft, from the F-5 Hawk to the X-1 Nova. Each has its own trade-off (paired or triple missiles and bombs, self-repair on the Viper and Specter) and its own five-level upgrade tracks.
  • Seven bosses, including the B-9 Stratofortress, the Leviathan Airship and the Crimson Ace dogfighter, each with an enraged second phase.
  • Hazards that hit both sides: lightning, falling lava bombs, rooftop torpedoes and watchtower searchlights that launch missiles.
  • Cannons, homing missiles and gravity bombs, a combo multiplier and saved progress.

How they're built

Both games share the same approach: small, fast and self-contained.

Generated in code

Every 3D model, texture, sound effect and music track is created at runtime. Apart from the app icons, there are no image or audio files to download.

Plays offline

A service worker precaches each game, so once it has loaded it runs without a connection. Updates install in the background.

Any device, any input

Touch, keyboard, mouse and gamepad, plus tilt steering in Tunnel Arcade. On phones they play full screen in landscape.

Built and shipped by CI

Three.js and Vite. GitHub Actions builds each game and deploys it to GitHub Pages on every push, after running Tunnel Arcade's unit tests.

Both games are installable Progressive Web Apps: open one on your phone and choose “Add to Home Screen” (or “Install app”) to play it like a native app, even without a connection.
